I am a Senior Research & Development Engineer at Sestek, where I work on speech processing, speech recognition, speech synthesis, speaker recognition, and audio classification, and on getting these models into production. I've spent the last years training and deploying speech models on real call-center audio.

I hold an MS in Computer Science from Hacettepe University, where my thesis focused on retrieving relevant segments from instructional cooking videos to improve their accessibility. Before that, I completed my BS at Hacettepe, where my final project, Flying Assistant, used deep neural networks and computer vision to help people with visual impairments navigate.
